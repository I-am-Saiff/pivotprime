Scentmatic UAE Market Entry · Financial Modelling

Sometimes the most valuable advice is: don't.

A UK fragrance tech company came to us asking whether UAE expansion made financial sense. We built a full cash-flow model — month by month, venue by venue, sensitivity scenario by scenario. The answer was honest: the way they planned to enter would lose them money. We showed them exactly why, built an alternative path that worked, and let them decide. They chose to wait. That decision saved them over £100,000 in committed capital — and left them with a complete roadmap ready when the time is right.

Founder-Led Business · Retail · Dubai

The founder was approving every decision. Progress was real but progress felt slow. The team had capability — it lacked clear ownership. We mapped what should and shouldn't escalate, then redesigned roles around judgement, not just task delivery.
