How we do it

One-to-one interviews with the people doing the work, not only the leadership team. Private conversations surface what people will not say in a room.

Process mapping end to end, then a facilitated workshop per function where the team walks the process on screen and stress tests it together. The findings are owned rather than imposed.

What happens after

The audit ends with a decision, not a filing cabinet. Some clients take the roadmap and execute it themselves, and that is a legitimate outcome. Most ask us to run some or all of it. We will tell you plainly which of those the findings actually justify.

The argument

We do not sprinkle AI over a business and call it transformation.

A business moves at the speed of its biggest constraint. Making everything else faster only builds a longer queue in front of it.

Work in progress
Speed up everything except the bottleneck and the queue in front of it simply gets longer. The business feels busier and delivers exactly as much as it did before.

The misconception

A plan built on twelve even months will miss badly.

Most businesses arrive with a home-market forecast. This market does not trade that way.

Ramadan reshapes trading for a month Summer takes a large part of your customer base out of the country Heat changes logistics, storage and footfall for half the year Q4 has to carry what the rest of the year cannot
A flat forecast assumes every month behaves the same way. Press the toggle to see what the year actually looks like, and what it does to a plan built elsewhere.
The numbers come first

There are hundreds of company formation experts in Dubai who will get you a licence and sometimes a bank account. We support all of that, but the licence is where we start rather than where we stop.

Before anything else we build a financial model with you: every assumption priced in, the investment required, the breakeven point and the expected return.

This is where most international businesses go wrong. They price for this market using home-market assumptions and end up busy but unprofitable.

  • The operating model, and the people to run it once you are live
