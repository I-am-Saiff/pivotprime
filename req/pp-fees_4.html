Chapter 04 — Fees

Most consultants charge whether it works or not.

The traditional model

You pay for the advice. Whether anything actually improves is not really their problem.

The invoice arrives either way.

The Pivot Prime model

We agree a target $400,000 in cost savings
Our fee structure 20% of savings delivered
You keep $320,000
We earn $80,000 — only when we deliver

Every engagement has a fixed element and a results element. A meaningful part of our fee sits on the other side of the agreed target being hit.

The commitment

If we haven't moved your numbers, most of our fee doesn't get paid.
