Chapter 04 — Fees

Most consultants charge whether it works or not.

Traditional consulting charges by the hour and the deck. You pay for the advice. Whether anything actually changes is someone else's problem.

We are a small, focused team — and we structured our fees to work differently. We only fully earn when you do.

How it works in practice

Say we agree your operational costs need to come down by $400,000. We commit to hitting that target. Our fee is a percentage of what we actually save you — so if we deliver $400K in savings, we take 20%. If we don't deliver, we don't earn it.

Performance-linked fees

The commitment

If we haven't moved your numbers, most of our fee doesn't get paid.

Before we start, we agree the target — a cost reduction, a margin improvement, a revenue number. A meaningful part of what we charge sits on the other side of that target being hit.
